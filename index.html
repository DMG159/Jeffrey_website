FAQ

Straight answers

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. Sessions come in blocks of 10, 20, or 30 and stay good for 12 months. Online training is month to month, or yearly with two months free. If you step away, your progress, messages, and history stay saved, ready when you come back.

Can I try it before I buy a block?

Yes. Book a $25 assessment with a coach. If you join, the $25 comes off your first purchase.

What is Online training?

Coaching in the app: meal planning, weekly check-ins, and your program, for $147 a month or $1,470 a year. Buy it on its own or add it to any session block.

What is digital coaching?

It's your coach in your pocket. Message them in the app between sessions, send a form-check video, ask what to do on your own days, and get a real answer back, usually within 24 hours. Everything stays in one place so your coach always knows where you are.

Can you train around an injury?

We can. We build around what your body can do today and add load as you earn it. For anything that needs medical care, we'll tell you straight and work alongside your physical therapist or doctor.

Do I need to be in shape already?

No. Wherever you're starting, new, getting back into it, or chasing a number, we meet you there. No judgment, no ego, just the next right step.
